Remedi Super Admin

Account & Data Deletion Request

This page explains how to request deletion of your Remedi Super Admin account and/or associated data.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email from your registered email address to:

support@remedi.jp
Compose Deletion Email

In the email, include your registered email, account ID (if known), and whether you want account deletion or deletion of specific data only.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account information (name, email address, account ID, role/permissions)
  • Authentication data (password hash, sessions/tokens)
  • In-app settings/data linked to your account

Data We May Retain

For legal compliance, security, and fraud prevention, we may retain certain records for a limited period:


  • Security and audit logs (for fraud detection and service security)
  • Billing/payment records required by law (if applicable)
  • Backups (for disaster recovery)

Processing Time (Estimated)

  • Deletion requests are typically processed within 30 days.
  • Data that exists in backups may be retained for up to 90 days before being overwritten/removed.
  • Records required by law may be retained for the legally required period.
Note: To check the status of your request, reply to the same email thread.
